### Verdict
FAIL

Two **REAL Majors** force FAIL under `severity × reality`: Finding 1 is the round-2 defect class reappearing through the trim path (a character of the replaced value survives under its replacement), and Finding 2 is a silent deletion of a non-replaced object's characters. Both were reproduced in this session through `PDFModifier.process`; both are **UNTESTED** in the sense that matters — the existing assertions cannot see them (a 1-character residue is below `surviving_fragments`'s window of 2, and line-level inclusion cannot see a mutilated line). The rubric agrees (Correctness C, Verification C, Reliability C). The positive half is real and was verified independently: the round-2 Major is genuinely closed on its shapes, `make check` is green at 400 passed / 84.65%, and the complexity decomposition holds under the repo's own tool.
